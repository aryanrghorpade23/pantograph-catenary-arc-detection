# References and attribution

## Primary dataset paper

D. Signorino, D. Giordano, A. Mariscotti, D. Gallo, A. Delle Femine, F. Balic, J. Quintana, L. Donadio, and A. Biancucci, “Dataset of measured and commented pantograph electric arcs in DC railways,” *Data in Brief*, vol. 31, article 105978, 2020. DOI: [10.1016/j.dib.2020.105978](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2020.105978).

The uploaded author-accepted manuscript identifies its licence as **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**. This repository does not redistribute that PDF.

## Dataset

J. Quintana et al., “Pantograph arcing in DC railway systems: Transient behavior of voltage and current recorded at pantograph,” Mendeley Data, version 1, 2020. DOI: [10.17632/74nz86wcgy.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/74nz86wcgy.1). Dataset landing page: <https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/74nz86wcgy/1>.

The primary recording used in the project is `MM_T_1.mat`. Obtain the dataset from Mendeley Data and follow its current access and reuse terms.

## Dataset attribution

The pantograph measurements were collected and published by the authors listed in the primary dataset paper. This repository is an educational signal-processing analysis of selected public data; it is not the source of the original railway measurements.
